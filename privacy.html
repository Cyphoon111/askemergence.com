Privacy

Last updated 26 September 2026. Ask Emergence is currently in private testing; this policy will be expanded before public release.

  • What we process. The questions you submit are sent to third-party AI providers (currently Anthropic and OpenAI) to produce your answer. Results are stored with your account so you can review past runs.
  • What we don't do. We do not sell your data and we do not use your questions to train models.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
  • Deletion. You can delete any run from within the app. To delete an account or ask a question, email support@askemergence.com.
