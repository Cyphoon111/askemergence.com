A debate, not a chat message

A single model answers in one unchecked call. An Ask Emergence run is about five calls across three frontier models, and you see every one of them.

1Position

Your question goes to the first intelligence, which commits to a complete plan on its own, before anyone else weighs in.

2Challenge

A second intelligence reviews it adversarially: objections, revisions, a competing plan. Disagreement forces another round.

3Audit

Agreement is not the end. A final auditor checks the agreed plan for material defects. If it finds one, it overrules both and decides.

Questions

What exactly is a "run"?

One question in, one audited answer out. Inside, a run is about five model calls: an independent position from one frontier model, an adversarial review by a second from a different lab, a verdict, and an audit by a third. If the models disagree twice, the auditor decides. Every call is listed in the answer's receipt.

Why does it cost more than a chat message?

Because it is five of them, across the most capable models available, with one of them acting as a check on the others. A run costs about what a deep-research task costs on other services, and unlike a chat message it comes with a review and an audit you can read.

Which models are used?

Today: GPT-6 Sol from OpenAI as the proposer, Claude Opus 5 as the reviewer, and Claude Fable 5.1 as the auditor and final judge. The mix is chosen from measured results, and we publish which model made each call in every receipt.

Does Emergence Engine minimize mistakes and hallucinations?

Yes. That is what the workflow is built for. A single model answers once, with nothing checking it. In an Emergence run the first model's plan is reviewed adversarially by a second frontier model from a different lab, which has to agree with it, and the agreed plan is then audited by a third model for material defects before it is released to you. An error has to get past two independent checks instead of none, and every answer comes with a receipt showing each call that was made. That is far more scrutiny than any single-model answer receives.

Do credits expire?

Monthly subscription credits reset at each renewal. Credits you buy separately never expire and work on any plan.

What is auto top-up?

One switch. When your credits fall below a level you choose, we add the amount you chose so a run is never interrupted. You're billed only when it fires, and you can turn it off any time.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from your account. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Is there an iPhone app?

Yes, and it's coming to the App Store. Until then Ask Emergence works fully in your browser at app.askemergence.com, on desktop and phone.

What happens to my questions?

They are sent to the AI providers to produce your answer and stored with your account so you can revisit past runs. We do not sell your data and do not use your questions to train models. See the privacy policy.
